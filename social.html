These are not the same number as yours. Reach, saves and shares are private — Instagram gives them for accounts you own and for nobody else. For other accounts the API gives likes and comments against their follower count, which is the engagement shown here. Views on their reels are read off their public page once a night (and when you press Refresh), because Instagram prints a reel's play count for anyone to see. What compares cleanly is how a post did against its own account's normal: that is what the flame means, on both sides of this toggle — on views where there are views, on engagement otherwise.
